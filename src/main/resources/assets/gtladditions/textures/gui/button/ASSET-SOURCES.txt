Dimension Folding toggle icons
==============================

dimension_fold_off.png and dimension_fold_on.png are unmodified copies of
GT New Horizons / GT5-Unofficial's TecTech safe_void_off.png and safe_void_on.png.
These are the icons used by the Dimensional Convergence button in the
Dimensionally Transcendent Plasma Forge (MTEPlasmaForgeGui).
