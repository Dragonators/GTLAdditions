# Building Gadgets

Copy and cut selections can now cover up to **one million blocks** at a time.

When you copy and paste GT machines, those that need a binding will automatically bind to you (goodbye, all wireless energy hatches).

You can now also use cut and paste to move existing machines while keeping their original binding, inventory and recipe progress.

Left-click a Wireless Energy Monitor while holding any gadget to bind it to your wireless energy network. Click again while it is bound to you to remove the binding and return to battery power.
