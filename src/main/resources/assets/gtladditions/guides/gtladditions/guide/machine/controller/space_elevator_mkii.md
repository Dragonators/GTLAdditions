# Space Elevator MKII

<BlockImage id="gtladditions:space_elevator_mkii" scale="8"/>

<Column gap="15" fullWidth={true}>

* <ItemLink id="gtladditions:space_elevator_mkii" /> can connect up to 56 space elevator modules.

## Advanced Elevator Modules

* [Astral Convergence Nexus](astral_convergence_nexus.md): processes space assembly module recipes.
* [Nebula Reaper](nebula_reaper.md): processes space mining and space drilling recipes.

## MKII Bonus

The advanced module tier is determined by the power module in the elevator structure. A regular space elevator grants `8^(module tier-1)` maximum parallel; when connected to <ItemLink id="gtladditions:space_elevator_mkii" />, this becomes `12^(module tier-1)`.
