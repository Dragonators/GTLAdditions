asteroid:shapes; asteroid:normals:{eros,itokawa,toutatis,geographos,golevka,kleopatra,mithra,hw1}
NASA-3D-Resources, "3D Printing" STL files for 433 Eros, 25143 Itokawa,
4179 Toutatis, 1620 Geographos, 6489 Golevka, 216 Kleopatra, 4486 Mithra
and 8567 (1996 HW1).
Source: https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources/tree/master/3D%20Printing
NASA's repository describes its assets as free and without copyright and links
to NASA's usage guidelines. The precise upstream lineage of these individual
STL files has not been established. Retained acknowledgement for radar models:
"3D asteroid model courtesy of Scott Hudson, Washington State University."
https://users.tricity.wsu.edu/~hudson/Research/Asteroids/models.html
Hudson's page states non-commercial/non-profit use with acknowledgement;
this notice does not assert that NASA's STL files were directly obtained there.

Processing: the handoff rejoined Eros/Itokawa print halves, resampled shapes
as radial fields (filling overhangs), and baked 80/320-triangle meshes and
object-space normal maps. Fine relief on the six radar/lightcurve shapes
comes from Bennu. Eros/Itokawa borrow surface mosaics from Bennu/Ceres/Vesta.
Mosaic polar gaps were mirrored, broad lighting flattened and contrast clipped.
The native renderer uses quality C: 256x128 textures, 136,000 triangles across the full belt.
Texture reduction uses box filtering from the supplied 1024x512 pixels.
Normals use RGB8 and grayscale mosaics use R8 with independent mipmaps.
Mesh reduction uses constrained quadric edge collapse; original axis extrema
and the farthest vertex are fixed, preserving bounds and maximum radius.
Spin rates use 0.46875 degrees/second steps.
Exporter: scripts/subspace_nexus/export_asteroid_belt.py.

transit:craft (transit lane: Gozanti-class cruiser, VT-49 Decimator and BFF-1
bulk freighter)
Author: DanielAndersson
Sources: "Star Wars: Gozanti-Class Cruiser",
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/6a587e545b1c48bea95480b49458ac63
"Star Wars: VT-49 Decimator",
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/3d9103b8d13942a3a990a0f42f2f4c4a
"Star Wars: BFF-1 Bulk Freighter",
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2131f750afff479588a46321d17ee25f
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: reduced in Blender (Decimate collapse), the Gozanti's pose baked and
its rig removed, scaled to a common size and repacked as native meshes whose
vertices carry their material values. Textures: the Gozanti's worn-metal and
normal pictures (ScratchedMetalDark) at 512 px, also laid on the VT-49's
untextured hull by object-space projection. The BFF-1's grey and blue scratched
metal is the same picture, the blue one as a tint in the vertex colour, and its
metal / roughness pictures are reduced to constants. The jump deformation,
engine glare, wake, shock wisps and ship trails are procedural GLSL.
scripts/prepare_transit_meshes.py merges byte-identical hull vertices without
changing triangle order and bakes the fixed wake and trail profiles.
