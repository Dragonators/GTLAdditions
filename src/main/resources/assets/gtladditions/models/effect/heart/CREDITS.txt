Starkiller base
  Reconstructed for this project from the public gallery images of chrikre's "Starkiller Base" (TurboSquid 976439); that commercial
  model was not obtained and none of its data is used.
  Ice: USGS Astrogeology Science Center, "Europa Voyager - Galileo SSI Global Mosaic 500m" (NASA / JPL / USGS; use constraints:
  none), its soft Voyager-era areas filled with fine detail taken from its own crisp areas, reduced to 4096 x 2048, with
  low-frequency tone and colour from Hermes, "Europa (2K)", Celestia Motherlode ("Free to use
  and edit"), reduced to 1024 x 512.
